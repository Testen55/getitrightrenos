Full Consultation — $150 to $500, credited toward your job

Want an exact, written price instead of a ballpark? Book a full consultation. The fee is quoted per project — between $150 and $500, plus HST — based on what your job needs: permits, drawings, special equipment, bringing in another expert, and distance. We confirm your fee in writing by email before anything is booked, and the fee is paid in advance by Interac e-Transfer to getitrightrenos@gmail.com. Your consultation is booked, and our team is dispatched, only once the fee is received — nobody is sent out before payment.

The fee is not an extra cost on top of your renovation: if you sign a contract with Get It Right Renovations within 90 days of your consultation and your project proceeds, the full fee is credited against your contract price. If you decide not to proceed, the fee is payment for the consultation itself. And if we decline your project, we will tell you before any fee is charged — there is no charge. Tick the full-consultation box on the form below and we'll quote your fee when we reply.

Your written quote is valid for 30 days from the date of issue. After that, the quote may be repriced at current rates.

Privacy

We use the information you send us through the consultation and newsletter forms on this site — your name, contact details and project details — only to respond to your request and prepare your estimate and quote, and, if you become a client, to carry out and document your project. If you ticked the offers box, we also use your email address to send you renovation tips and seasonal offers until you unsubscribe. We do not sell your information. Frequently Asked Questions

Do you handle permits?

Where a building, plumbing or electrical permit is required for your project, it is applied for before that work starts and inspections are booked as the work proceeds. Electrical work is done by a licensed electrical contractor and inspected by the Electrical Safety Authority; plumbing work is done by licensed plumbers.

Are you insured?

Yes — we are fully insured, and certificates are available on request.

Do you do commercial work?

Yes. We handle residential, commercial, and institutional projects including office renovations, tenant improvements, and maintenance contracts.

Can you help with government rebates?

Absolutely. We guide you through eligibility, help complete applications, and ensure your project qualifies for available federal and provincial incentives, including Ontario's Home Renovation Savings Program.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Peel and serve the Greater Toronto Area (GTA). Contact us to discuss your specific location.

How long does a typical project take?

Timelines vary by project scope. We provide detailed schedules during the consultation phase and always communicate any changes upfront.
