About Us

More than a renovation — it's an education in building right.

Our Story

Founded by Matthew Martin, Get It Right Renovations Inc. is a unionized (Local 27 Carpenters), Black-owned renovation company based in Peel and proudly serving the Greater Toronto Area. We are not a cheap handyman service. We fix structural, moisture, and performance problems properly — not cosmetically. Every project reflects our commitment to doing it right the first time.

Owner Matthew Martin is a Local 27 carpenter, and Get It Right Renovations Inc. is a signatory to Local 27 — a unionized company. That means trained, certified union craftsmanship on your project, and a company that stands behind its work.

Why Choose Us?

  • Union Local 27 Carpenters – Access to the highest standards of craftsmanship
  • Transparent Pricing – No hidden costs. Detailed written quotes with clear scope definition
  • Code-Compliant Execution – Work is performed to the Ontario Building Code and the municipal requirements that apply to your project. Where a permit is required, work proceeds under that permit and inspections are completed as required.
  • Fully Insured – Certificates available on request
  • Government Rebate Expertise – Guidance on rebate qualification for energy-efficient upgrades
  • Sustainability & Durability – Long-term performance and energy efficiency at our core
