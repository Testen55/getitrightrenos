Government Rebates & Incentives

We help you access government rebates for energy-efficient upgrades. We guide you through eligibility, paperwork, and the application process so you get every dollar available.

Ontario homeowners may be eligible for rebates through the Home Renovation Savings Program — $100 per opening for ENERGY STAR® windows and doors, plus rebates for insulation and air sealing, up to $5,000 for gas-heated homes or $10,000 for homes heated with electricity, oil, propane or wood.†

†Rebates are provided by the Home Renovation Savings Program (Enbridge Gas and Save on Energy, with support from the Government of Ontario), not by Get It Right Renovations. A home energy assessment is required before work begins, at least two eligible upgrades must be completed, and eligibility, amounts and deadlines are set by the program and subject to change. The former Canada Greener Homes Grant is closed to new applicants.
